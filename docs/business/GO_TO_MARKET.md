# Go-to-market one-pager (Phase 19)

**Who first:** Indian SME exporters of handicrafts, home textiles and gifts selling to UAE buyers over WhatsApp,
10–200 enquiries a month, owner-led, no ERP. First user: the founder's family business in Jaipur (pilot).

**Why they buy:** minutes per quote (baseline interview), fewer document mistakes before shipment, CEPA savings shown
with citations, and control: nothing leaves without the owner.

**Channels (in order):**
1. Design partners from the pilot and the founder's network (target: 3 letters of intent, `docs/pilot/LOI_TEMPLATE.md`).
2. Export promotion councils and trade bodies (EPCH for handicrafts, regional exporter associations) — demo days.
3. Dubai side: Dubai Chamber programmes, buyer agents and forwarders who receive these enquiries.
4. Content: short bilingual videos of a WhatsApp voice note becoming an approved quote.

**First 10 customers plan:** pilot (1) → design partners (3) → council demo day (3) → referrals from partners (3).
Measure: trial → paid conversion, enquiries per customer, minutes saved (pilot suite), churn after 90 days.

**Pricing:** INR monthly plans (`/pricing`), validated in the five interviews; annual discount only after retention
data exists. Payments go live after company registration (Stripe or a local gateway).

**Risks:** wrong HS/origin advice (mitigated by citations, "needs human check", adviser review), WhatsApp policy
changes (approved templates), model costs (measured per quote, budget cap), a single founder (documented system,
tests, CI).
